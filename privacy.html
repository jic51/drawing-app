Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

SketchSend is a messaging app where every message is a drawing. This page explains what we store, why, and how long we keep it. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than legal boilerplate.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or store your location.
  • We do not read your phone's contacts.
  • We do not access your camera or photo library.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not show advertising.

Your drawings

A drawing you send to a friend is stored so they can open it. Twenty-four hours later it is deleted, automatically and irreversibly.

A drawing you choose to share to our public gallery works differently: it stays up for as long as we keep the gallery, and does not expire. You can ask us to take yours down at any time.

Anyone who has the link to a drawing can open it. Links are long and random, so they cannot be guessed, but a person who receives a drawing can pass its link to someone else. Treat a drawing as something that can be shared onward, like a photo you send in a chat.

Services we rely on

We do not run our own servers. The app is built on these services, and your data passes through them:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database and notifications.
  • PostHog — anonymous usage statistics.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the app itself.

Your choices

  • You can change your name and @username at any time in Settings.
  • You can turn notifications off in Settings or in your browser.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything attached to it.
  • You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you.

For any of the above, or any question about this page, write to hello@sketchsend.app.

Age

SketchSend is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes to this page

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced inside the app.
